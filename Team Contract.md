## 3. Communication Channels and Response-Time Expectations

The team has agreed to use the following communication channels:

* **Email** – For formal communication, important project information, documents, and communication with the advisor or instructor.
* **Text Message + Teams Message** – For quick communication, scheduling, reminders, and time-sensitive team updates.
* **Teams** – For remote team meetings, discussions, and advisor meetings when meeting in person is not practical.

Team members are expected to respond to messages **within 24 hours whenever reasonably possible**. If a team member is unable to provide a complete response within that period, they should at minimum acknowledge the message and explain the reason for the delay.

Team members should communicate anticipated periods of unavailability when possible so that the rest of the team can plan accordingly.

## 4. Meeting Cadence

### Team Meetings

The team will hold **one to two team-only meetings per week** depending on the project's current workload and upcoming deadlines.

Additional meetings may be scheduled when necessary, particularly before major milestones, presentations, demonstrations, or submission deadlines.

### Advisor Meetings

The team will aim to meet with the project advisor **weekly or bi-weekly**, depending on the advisor's availability and the team's current needs.

The purpose of advisor meetings will be to provide progress updates, discuss challenges, receive feedback, and ensure the project remains on track.

---

## 5. Decision-Making Process and Tiebreaker

**Decision-Making Process:**

--- The team will attempt to make any decisions through discussion and have general agreement amongst all members. Each team member will have the opportunity to share their opinions before a decision is made.

---For routine decisions, especially if they're related to a person's assigned functional role, the team will allow that person to lead in that area while still considering feedback from the rest of the team.

---If the team cannot reach a general agreement after discussion, the issue will be decided with a majority vote.

**Tiebreaker Procedure:**

--- If a vote results in a tie, then the team will to discuss the issue again, and attempt to reach some sort of compromise.

--- If the team is still unable to reach a decision, the issue can be brought to our advisor, David Salem, for his input or from Professor Zhan.

---

## 6. Conflict-Resolution and Escalation Plan

The team will attempt to resolve disagreements and conflicts professionally and internally before seeking outside intervention.

The following escalation process will be used:

1. **Direct Discussion:** Problems should first be discussed between the team members directly involved. The goal should be to understand each person's concerns and reach a reasonable solution.

2. **Team Discussion:** If the individuals involved cannot resolve the issue, or if the issue affects the entire team, it should be brought to the full team for discussion.

3. **Advisor Involvement:** If the team cannot reach a satisfactory resolution internally or believes outside input would be helpful, the issue will be brought to the team's **Capstone advisor** for guidance.

4. **Instructor Escalation:** If the issue remains unresolved after advisor involvement, the team may escalate the matter to the **Capstone instructor, Professor Zhan**, for further guidance or resolution.

Team members agree to approach conflicts respectfully, focus discussions on the project and relevant behavior rather than personal issues, and make a good-faith effort to resolve disagreements at the lowest appropriate level.
